The NO SUS wordmark: "NO SUS" in heavy, tightly tracked Geist, white, followed by a small #808080 square at 0.21em.

## Usage

```tsx
<NoSusLogo sizeClass="text-lg md:text-xl" />
```

- Size it with Tailwind font-size classes in `sizeClass` (`text-lg` in the navbar and footer). The square scales with the text.
- The wordmark is white, so place it on the brand black (`bg-brand-black`, #080808) or another dark ground.
- Do not recolor the square, add effects, or set it in another font.
